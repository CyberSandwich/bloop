bloop puts your files on a shared screen from your phone. This page lists what it does today and what comes next.

## Present From Your Phone {#present}

Scan the QR code on the screen with your phone's camera. Your phone shows three numbers; tap the one the screen shows, and your files open in order. In the demo, choose your files and tap Approve.

## Your Library {#library}

Add your slides, documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, photos and videos once, from your phone or your computer. bloop prepares each one to show in the browser, so a deck still shows on a PC without PowerPoint.

Your files stay yours: kept in the EU, and deleted when you say. A deleted file waits in Trash first, so you can restore it ([how long](/privacy#retention)).

## Up Next {#up-next}

Mark the files for your next talk as Up Next, at your desk. At the lectern, one tap on the right number opens them.

## On the Screen {#screen}

The screen shows slides, PDFs, spreadsheets, documents, photos and videos in its browser. A deck opens in PowerPoint when the PC has it. Your speaker notes show only on your phone.

## You Stay in Charge {#charge}

- The screen gets a link to each item you approve, never your account.
- End from your phone at any time, and each link stops working.
- If you forget, it ends by itself when its time runs out.
- A downloaded copy can stay on the PC. [See what can stay](/security#stay).

## Sign In With an Invite {#sign-in}

Accounts are by invite during early access. You sign in with a code from your email, and you can add a passkey if your device offers one. [Join the waitlist]({{waitlist}}?source=features) to get an invite.

Privacy Notice

This notice is a draft until bloop's legal review.

What the Screen Gets

  • The items you approve
  • A link to each, which expires

What the Screen Never Gets

  • Your accountNever
  • Your other filesNever

Who Is Responsible

bloop runs this service. Write to hi@bloop.here with any question about your data.

What bloop Keeps

What bloop keeps and for how long
Your email and account IDWhile you stay connected, then 30 days.
Your OneDrive access, encryptedUntil you disconnect (deleted at once) or remove bloop in My Apps or Entra (deleted the next time it fails), or 30 days unused.
A log of who opened which file, in which room, and when180 days. For the demo, 7 days.
A guest's fileDeleted when the talk ends, and after 1 day at most.
Your phone's sign-in30 days unused, 90 days at most.
Usage counts, with no names90 days, then daily totals for 2 years.
Waitlist: the email and answers you sendKept to invite you to early access, until you ask for them to be deleted or the waitlist closes.
Waitlist: who handles the formTally (tally.so) runs the form for bloop. It collects your role, where you would use bloop, your organization, your next step, your timing and your email, and keeps them until you ask for deletion or the waitlist closes.

bloop keeps no slide content from your OneDrive. A guest's file is the one exception, and it is deleted when the talk ends.

Why

To open the file you approve on the screen you choose, to keep the service safe, and to give your organization's IT team a record of what was opened where.

Where

Your data is stored in the EU on Cloudflare, except waitlist answers, which Tally holds. Cloudflare is a US company, and each request is handled at its nearest data center.

Your Choices

Once OneDrive is connected, tap Disconnect OneDrive on any phone while you approve: bloop deletes its stored access and every sign-in ends at once, a lost phone's included. You may also be able to remove bloop in My Apps (myapplications.microsoft.com); your IT team can remove it for everyone in Microsoft Entra. To see or erase what bloop holds about you, write to the address above.

Questions

What is bloop?

A way to open your slides on a shared screen, such as a lecture room PC, from your phone. The screen gets only the file you choose.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The screen runs bloop in its web browser, and your phone uses its camera. The room PC opens your slides in PowerPoint.

What does the room PC keep?

A link to the file you open, never your account. A downloaded copy or the file name in recent lists can stay until the PC's profile is wiped. See what can stay.

Is it free?

Yes. Early access is free while bloop is being tested.

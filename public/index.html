Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while bloop is in early access.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The screen opens bloop in its browser, and your phone uses its camera.

What stays on the room PC?

Only a link to each item you open, and never your account. A downloaded copy or the browser's history can stay until the PC is wiped. See what can stay.

Which files work?

PowerPoint, PDF, photos, videos and links.

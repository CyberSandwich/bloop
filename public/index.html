Questions

What is bloop?

A way to present your files on a shared screen, such as a lecture room PC, from your phone. The screen gets only the items you approve.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The screen runs bloop in its web browser, and your phone uses its camera. The room PC opens slides in PowerPoint.

What does the room PC keep?

A link to each item you open, never your account. A downloaded copy or a file name in recent lists can stay until the PC's profile is wiped. See what can stay.

Is it free?

Yes. Early access is free while bloop is being tested.
